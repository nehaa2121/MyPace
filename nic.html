National Informatics Centre

National Informatics Centre is the technology arm of the Government of India under the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY). It was established in 1976 and develops and maintains many of the country's most important digital government systems.


What does NIC do?

NIC builds and manages:
  • Government websites and portals
  • e-Governance applications
  • Digital India projects
  • National data centres
  • Government cloud (MeghRaj)
  • Cybersecurity and secure government networks
  • Software for ministries, states, districts, and public services

Examples of systems supported by NIC include:

  • Digital government portals
  • District administration websites
  • Online citizen service platforms
  • Secure government communication systems

Work you may do

As a software engineer/scientist at NIC, you might:

  • Develop Java, Python, or .NET applications
  • Design databases (Oracle, PostgreSQL, MySQL)
  • Work on cloud infrastructure
  • Build APIs and web applications
  • Improve cybersecurity
  • Support AI and data analytics projects
  • Maintain critical government IT systems

Why many students aim for NIC

  • Central Government Group A technical career (for Scientist posts)
  • Good salary with government benefits
  • Strong work-life balance compared with many private IT jobs
  • Opportunity to work on systems used by millions of citizens
  • Stable career with opportunities for promotion


Since you've completed BCA and are planning to do MCA, your best path to NIC (National Informatics Centre) is:

BCA → MCA → GATE CSE → NIC Scientist-B

NIC Scientist-B recruitment now shortlists candidates using valid GATE scores followed by document verification/interview, rather than the older written examination process.
