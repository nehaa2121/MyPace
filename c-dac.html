Centre for Development of Advanced Computing (C-DAC)

C-DAC is a premier R&D organization under the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY), Government of India.

It works on advanced technologies such as:

  • Supercomputers
  • Artificial Security
  • Cyber Security
  • Cloud Computing
  • Embedded Systems
  • Healthcare IT
  • High-Performance Computing (HPC)
  • Quantum Computing

If you love technology and want a government-sector IT career, C-DAC is one of the top organizations in India.

What Does C-DAC Do?

  • Supercomputers:
    C-DAC developed India's famous supercomputer series:
    • PARAM 8000
    • PARAM Siddhi-AI
  • Cyber Security:
    • Security tools
    • Digital forensics
    • Malware analysis
    • Secure communication systems
  • Software Development:
    • Government portals
    • Healthcare systems
    • Educational platforms
  • Electronics & Embedded Systems:
    • IOT
    • Robotics
    • VLSI
    • Embedded Software

Can BCA Students Apply?

Yes, for some C-DAC project-based recruitments, BCA graduates may be eligible if the notification specifically allows it.

However, many higher technical/ Scientist roles prefer:

  • MCA
  • B.E./B.Tech
  • M.Tech
  • Relevant postgraduate qualification
