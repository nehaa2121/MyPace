How take GATE

Check Eligibility

GATE is open to students in eligible undergraduate or postgraduate programs. As an MCA Student, you can apply if the notification for your year includes your program.

Register Online

When registration open (typically once a year)

  • Visit the official GATE website announced for that year's exam.
  • Create an Account
  • Upload
    • Passport-size photograph
    • Signature
    • Required identity proof
    • Educational details/documents as specified
    • Pay the application fee

Choose the Paper

For a career in NIC scientist - B
Choose: Computer Science & Information Technology (CS)

Download your Admit Card

A few weeks before the exam, download your admit card from the official portal.

Take the Exam

GATE is a computer-based test (CBT) conducted at designed exam centers.
The computer science (CS) paper includes topics such as:

  • Engineering Mathematics
  • Digital Logic
  • Computer Organization
  • Programming & Data Structures
  • Algorithms
  • OS
  • DBMS
  • Computer NEtworks
  • Theory of Computation
  • Compiler Design

Get Your GATE Score

After the results are declared, you'll receive your score. You can use it for NIC Scientist-B recruitment

Goal:

  • 2026 - 2028 Complete MCA
  • During MCA 1st & 2 yr Prepare for GATE CS take GATE in your final year of MCA so you have a valid score when applying for NIC recruitment
